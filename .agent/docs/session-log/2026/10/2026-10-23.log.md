### Decisions made
- Prefix per app rather than only fixing the reader: Better Auth's own
  `getSessionCookie` also prefers `__Secure-`, so a foreign app's secure cookie still wins
  unless names differ.
- Do not delete legacy `better-auth.*` cookies — on localhost they may belong to another
  app (maprios). The rename signs every user out once, including on vexcms.dev.
- Did not patch Better Auth's `anonymous()` after-hook wipe (sign-in carrying a dead
  signed cookie returns 200 with `session_token` Max-Age=0); the working proxy delete
  now removes most dead cookies before sign-in.
- maprios-app left untouched (separate repo); vex is isolated regardless.

### Problems hit
- Sign-in looped back to `/auth/sign-in` until site data was cleared. Root cause:
  maprios-app on `localhost:3007` issues `__Secure-better-auth.session_token` (dynamic
  `baseURL` object → `isProduction` → secure prefix); cookies don't isolate by port; the
  vex proxy read that name first, failed `getSessionWithUser`, and on redirect deleted
  vex's own valid cookie while its `__Secure-` delete lacked `Secure` and was ignored.
  Confirmed via Convex logs (01:45–01:47 UTC 10-22), session-table bursts on 10-16/10-18,
  and a Chromium repro; fixed by the prefix + reader + secure-delete changes, verified in
  Chromium (both apps reach `/admin` with the maprios cookie present).
- Live proxy (vexcms.dev) emitted the same non-Secure `__Secure-` delete — fixed by the
  same change once deployed.
