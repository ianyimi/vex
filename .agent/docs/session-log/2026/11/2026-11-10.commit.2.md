fix(react): filter builder scroll, OR groups on top, last-row clear

**Scrolling.** The Filter popover wrapped its groups in `ScrollSection` (Base UI `ScrollArea`).
Inside a popover the `max-height` never reaches `ScrollArea`'s viewport, so the panel showed a
scrollbar that did nothing and the conditions spilled past its bottom edge.
`ListViewFilterBuilder` now uses a plain `overflow-y-auto` container. Its max height is
`max-h-[506px]`, enough for the OR button plus about 3.25 single-row OR groups, and the
condition list scrolls once it is taller than that. The popover opens 8px below the Filter
button, so it is level with the top of the table frame. To support that gap,
`DataTableResponsiveMenu` gains an optional `sideOffset` prop, passed through to the desktop
`PopoverContent` (default `4`, so every other menu is unchanged).

**OR groups stack on top.** The OR button moved from the bottom of the condition list to the
top, and `handleAddGroup` puts the new group first. A new OR group always appears directly under
the button and the existing groups move down, so it is never added out of view at the bottom.
Filter conditions are now stored newest group first. Results are unchanged, because OR doesn't
depend on order, but the chips and the `filter=` URL list conditions in that order.

**The last condition can't be removed.** Previously the × on the only remaining condition
removed it and left the open builder with zero conditions. Now, when one condition is left,
`ListViewFilterRow` gets `removeMode="clear"`. Its × is labelled "Clear condition" and resets the
row to the fresh default (first readable field, its first operator, no value). It is disabled
(`removeDisabled`) while the row is still that default. With two or more conditions × removes,
as before.

**Tests and docs.** New regression test: the only condition's × starts disabled, enables after
typing, clears without removing the row, and is disabled again. The "removing a chip keeps a
still-open incomplete row" test now looks for the new group at index 0. The filter-builder
section of `guides/list-views.mdx` describes OR placement, the last-condition × and scrolling.
Changeset: `list-filter-builder-scroll-or-clear` (`@vexcms/react` patch). Templates: no `apps/*`
change, nothing to mirror.
